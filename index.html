The Garage

Our Story

Built on a passion for drag racing, mechanics, and fabrication, J Custom Garage started from the ground up—literally building our own shop equipment from salvaged metal. Today, that same hands-on mindset drives our specialized build space.

Our approach is simple: we don't skip steps. Whether it’s precision paintwork or a complete engine rebuild, we focus on the details so the work speaks for itself..
